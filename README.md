# Teddy & Bunny — English Adventures

A playful English-speaking playground for a young child who understands basic English and is growing in confidence using short phrases and starting conversations.

## The three games

1. **Story Time** — Teddy and Bunny have little adventures. The child suggests what happens next in her own words. There is no single correct answer.
2. **Everyday English** — learn a full set of practical phrases first, then match them to everyday situations, say them aloud, and try again without the model.
3. **Speak & Play** — the child practises starting a conversation, asking a question, reacting to a friend, and taking another turn. The goal is to practise initiating communication, not just answering adult questions.

## Voice and accessibility

- The site chooses an available English system voice, preferring voices whose names indicate a natural/neural option.
- Use the **Speaking voice** selector to choose another installed English voice.
- Voice quality is provided by the browser/device. Some devices only offer robotic voices; the site cannot create a studio-quality voice without a separate hosted text-to-speech service.
- Speech recognition is optional and may not be available or accurate for a young child's voice. It is never used as a score or test.
- An adult should supervise microphone permissions.
- The site does not save speech transcripts.
- Reduced-motion settings are respected.

## Publish with GitHub Pages

1. Open **Settings → Pages** in the repository.
2. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
3. Select branch **main** and folder **/(root)**, then click **Save**.
4. Wait for deployment. The URL will look like `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/English-game/`.

## Teaching approach

Give her time to think. A word, a short phrase, a gesture or a mixed-language attempt can all be a start. Model natural English without asking for perfection. The goal is happy, meaningful communication and growing confidence.
